# D&D 5E Asset Index

Where to get every kind of asset a 5E game needs — battlemaps, tokens, art, stat data, handouts, VTT libraries, audio, and generators — and what each source lets you do with it.

This file is a **directory of links**. Commercial and Patreon art/maps are linked, never mirrored: the files stay with their creators. Genuinely open data sets (SRD, Open5e, 5e-database) are marked **data importable (open license)** — those you can pull down and build on.

**Already imported:** the private companion repo `d123d/dnd` already holds local copies of the 5e-database JSON, the 5e-srd-api data set, the 5etools data, and the SRD 5.2.1 files — check there before importing anything twice.

**Already in your stores:** the Google Drive `dnd-data` folder holds a `reference-art` library organized by source — Baldur's Gate 3, *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, *Dungeon of the Mad Mage*, *Tales from the Yawning Portal* — plus map and PDF collections and an `assets/fonts` folder. This index is the online end of those same shelves: where the files came from, and where to get more.

Access key: **Free** / **Freemium** (free tier + paid) / **Patreon** / **Paid**. License notes are the source's own terms, in one line — check the site before any commercial use.

## Maps

Battlemaps, world/region maps, and the tools that make them.

- [Dyson Logos](https://dysonlogos.blog/) — 1,600+ hand-drawn dungeon, town and wilderness maps. *Free. His commercial-use map set is under a free royalty-free license with credit; the rest is free for personal use.*
- [2-Minute Tabletop](https://2minutetabletop.com/) — Hand-drawn battlemaps and a huge token library. *Freemium: base map packs pay-what-you-want, token library free.*
- [Czepeku](https://www.czepeku.com/) — Cinematic, richly detailed battlemaps, many with animated and variant versions. *Patreon / Paid. License tiers on the site separate personal from commercial use.*
- [Forgotten Adventures](https://www.forgotten-adventures.net/) — Battlemaps plus thousands of mapmaking objects and textures for Foundry/Roll20/DungeonDraft. *Free Core Mapmaking Pack; assets free for personal use, commercial terms on the site.*
- [Tom Cartos](https://www.tomcartos.com/) — Battlemaps, towns and complete location sets. *Patreon / Freemium — free maps on the site, full archive for patrons.*
- [Neutral Party](https://www.patreon.com/neutralparty) — Clean top-down battlemaps in a crisp, readable style. *Patreon, with free sample maps.*
- [Dungeon Mapster](https://www.patreon.com/dungeonmapster) — Hand-drawn battlemaps, gridded and gridless. *Patreon.*
- [Afternoon Maps](https://www.patreon.com/afternoonmaps) — Hand-drawn battlemaps and dungeon maps. *Patreon.*
- [Daniel's Maps](https://www.danielsmaps.com/) — Hand-drawn dungeon and encounter maps. *Free maps on the site, more via Patreon.*
- [Mike Schley](https://prints.mikeschley.com/) — The official D&D cartographer's own store: digital maps and prints from published adventures. *Paid, per-map license.*
- [Deven Rue](https://www.devenrue.com/) — Hand-drawn world and region maps in an antique style, plus custom commissions. *Paid / Patreon.*
- [Inkarnate](https://inkarnate.com/) — Browser map-making tool for world, region and battlemaps. *Freemium; the Pro tier carries a commercial license.*
- [DungeonDraft](https://dungeondraft.net/) — Desktop battlemap editor by Megasploot, built for VTT export. *Paid, one-time.*
- [Wonderdraft](https://www.wonderdraft.net/) — Desktop world-map editor for campaign-scale maps. *Paid, one-time.*
- [Dungeon Scrawl](https://www.dungeonscrawl.com/) — Browser dungeon-drawing tool with VTT and print export. *Free, with a Pro tier.*
- [Dungeon Fog](https://www.dungeonfog.com/) — Battlemap editor with a built-in map library and VTT features. *Freemium.*
- [Arkenforge](https://arkenforge.com/) — Map builder and VTT toolkit with a large built-in asset library. *Paid.*
- [Dungeon Alchemist](https://www.dungeonalchemist.com/) — AI-assisted battlemap generator: draw rooms, it furnishes them. *Paid (Steam).*
- [One Page Dungeon Contest](https://www.dungeoncontest.com/) — The annual contest archive: hundreds of complete one-page dungeons with maps. *Free; entries under their authors' terms.*

## Tokens & Art

Character and monster tokens, paper minis, portraits, and miniature designers.

- Forgotten Adventures — the token half of the map library above: thousands of top-down creature and character tokens on the [same site](https://www.forgotten-adventures.net/). *Free for personal use.*
- [Devin Night — Immortal Nights](https://immortalnights.com/) — Top-down character and monster token packs, a VTT staple since the MapTool era. *Paid packs plus free packs; personal use.*
- [Printable Heroes](https://printableheroes.com/) — Printable paper miniatures and matching VTT tokens for monsters and heroes. *Free / pay-what-you-want, with a Patreon for the full set.*
- [Paper Forge](https://www.patreon.com/paperforge) — Printable paper minis and tokens in a bold, readable style. *Patreon.*
- [Retrograde Minis](https://retrogrademinis.com/) — Old-school-style paper minis and tokens, plus a free custom token/mini creator. *Free creator; packs via Patreon and stores.*
- [Token Stamp — Jan Loos / Roll Advantage](https://rolladvantage.com/tokenstamp/) — Turn any portrait image into a framed round token in the browser. *Free.*
- [TokenTool — RPTools](https://www.rptools.net/toolbox/token-tool/) — Desktop token maker from the MapTool team: drag in art, add a border, export. *Free, open source.*
- [Hero Forge](https://www.heroforge.com/) — Design a custom character in 3D; buy the physical mini, the STL, or a digital token version. *Free to design; downloads Paid and personal-use only under their terms.*
- [Eldritch Foundry](https://eldritch-foundry.com/) — 3D custom miniature designer, Hero Forge's main rival, with STL downloads. *Freemium / Paid.*
- [Cartography Assets](https://cartographyassets.com/) — Marketplace for mapmaking assets and token packs, including free Forgotten Adventures packs. *Freemium marketplace; per-asset terms.*

## Stats & Structured Data

Importable 5E data: bestiaries, spells, items, and the tools that author stat blocks.

- [5etools](https://5e.tools/) — The full browser reference suite: bestiary, spells, classes, items, DM screen, loot. *Free fan tool; data importable into Foundry/Roll20 through its import tools.*
- [5etools source mirror](https://github.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-src) — The 5etools code and data on GitHub, for running a local/offline copy. *Code MIT; content is fan-compiled reference data, not open content.*
- [Open5e](https://open5e.com/) — Open reference site for SRD and open third-party 5E content. *Free; content OGL / CC as marked per source.*
- [Open5e API](https://api.open5e.com/) — REST API over the Open5e data set: monsters, spells, items, classes. *Free. Data importable (open license).*
- [Open5e on GitHub](https://github.com/open5e/open5e) — Site source; the data itself lives in the [open5e-api repo](https://github.com/open5e/open5e-api). *Open source.*
- [D&D 5e API](https://www.dnd5eapi.co/) — REST + GraphQL API for SRD 5E data, no auth, no key. *Free. Data importable (SRD, CC-BY-4.0).*
- [5e-database](https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database) — The JSON data set behind the D&D 5e API. *MIT code; SRD data. Data importable (open license) — already imported in d123d/dnd.*
- [SRD downloads — D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd) — Official SRD 5.1 and SRD 5.2.1 PDFs plus the conversion guide. *Free. CC-BY-4.0. Data importable (open license).*
- [Avrae](https://avrae.io/) — Discord bot for 5E play with a built-in SRD compendium and homebrew/import support. *Free.*
- [Foundry VTT — dnd5e system](https://foundryvtt.com/packages/dnd5e) — The official 5E system for Foundry, with SRD compendium packs ready to import; source at [foundryvtt/dnd5e](https://github.com/foundryvtt/dnd5e). *System MIT; compendium content SRD CC-BY-4.0.*
- [Roll20 5E Compendium](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e) — Drag-and-drop SRD compendium for Roll20 sheets. *Free for SRD; published-book compendiums Paid.*
- [The Homebrewery](https://homebrewery.naturalcrit.com/) — Write Markdown, get documents and stat blocks that look like official 5E books. *Free.*
- [GMBinder](https://www.gmbinder.com/) — Homebrew document and stat block builder in the official style, with PDF export. *Free tier.*
- [TetraCube Stat Block Generator](https://tetra-cube.com/dnd/dnd-statblock.html) — Monster stat block builder with JSON import/export and printable output. *Free.*

## Spells, Items & Card Sets

Spell and item data sets, and turning them into table-ready cards.

- [Open5e API data set](https://github.com/open5e/open5e-api) — The spells, magic items, monsters and more behind Open5e, as structured data. *Open source. Data importable (open license).*
- 5e-database — every SRD spell and item as JSON; listed under [Stats & Structured Data](#stats--structured-data), and already imported in d123d/dnd.
- [Hardcodex](https://hardcodex.ru/) — Spell and magic-item card generator (English/Russian): pick from SRD data or your own, print decks. *Free.*
- [RPG Cards](https://mephitrpg.github.io/rpg-cards/) — The maintained fork of crobi's card generator: define spell, item and monster cards as JSON, export printable PDFs. *Free, open source.*
- Single-file SRD spell JSON — [vorpalhex/srd_spells](https://github.com/vorpalhex/srd_spells), listed under Open Data in the [README](README.md). *Data importable (SRD).*

## Handouts, Fonts & Printables

Things to put in players' hands.

- [Raging Swan Press](https://www.ragingswanpress.com/) — GM resources, dungeon dressing and handouts, with a large free-sample library. *Free samples / Paid books.*
- [The Alexandrian](https://thealexandrian.net/) — Justin Alexander's GM archive: handouts, scenario structures, and the Three Clue Rule. *Free.*
- [Sly Flourish](https://slyflourish.com/) — Lazy DM advice and the free Lazy GM's Resource Document. *Free articles / Paid books.*
- [DMs Guild](https://www.dmsguild.com/) — The official community marketplace: handouts, maps, adventures. *Paid, with a large free / pay-what-you-want section.*
- [DriveThruRPG](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/) — The biggest RPG PDF marketplace, including WotC's back catalog and free titles. *Paid / free titles.*
- [DaFont — Fantasy fonts](https://www.dafont.com/theme.php?cat=403) — Fantasy display fonts for handouts, maps and titles. *Per-font licenses — many free for personal use, check each.*
- RPG Cards — printable spell and item cards for handouts; listed under [Spells, Items & Card Sets](#spells-items--card-sets).

## VTT Asset Libraries & Modules

Where VTT-ready asset packs and modules live.

- [Foundry VTT](https://foundryvtt.com/) — The platform most of these libraries target. *Paid, one-time license.*
- [Foundry package library](https://foundryvtt.com/packages/) — The official directory of systems, modules and world packs. *Mixed free / paid.*
- [JB2A — Jules & Ben's Animated Assets](https://jb2a.com/) — Animated spell and attack effects for Foundry. *Free asset subset; full library via Patreon.*
- [Moulinette](https://www.moulinette.cloud/) — Search and import maps, tokens, audio and tiles from many creators straight into Foundry. *Freemium; indexes Patreon creator libraries you support.*
- [Owlbear Rodeo](https://www.owlbear.rodeo/) — Lightweight browser VTT: bring your own maps and tokens, or use its extensions. *Free tier.*
- [TaleSpire](https://talespire.com/) — 3D VTT built from placeable miniature terrain slabs and minis. *Paid (Steam).*
- [Roll20 Marketplace](https://marketplace.roll20.net/) — Maps, token packs, tiles and ready modules for Roll20. *Paid, some free.*
- Cartography Assets — the map-asset marketplace; listed under [Tokens & Art](#tokens--art).

## Audio Ambience & Music

- [Tabletop Audio](https://tabletopaudio.com/) — Original 10-minute ambiences and music for fantasy, horror, sci-fi and nature scenes. *Free to stream and use at the table; see the site for commercial terms.*
- [Syrinscape](https://syrinscape.com/) — Soundset app with official D&D adventure sound packs and a huge fantasy library. *Subscription, with free content to start.*
- [Michael Ghelfi Studios](https://michaelghelfi.com/) — Film-quality RPG ambience and music; a large free catalog on YouTube and Bandcamp. *Free streaming / Paid packs and licenses.*
- [Monument Studios](https://www.monumentstudios.net/) — Cinematic fantasy music libraries built for RPG sessions (the Fantasy Complete collection). *Paid licenses.*

## Generators

Assets on demand: maps, dungeons, cities, names, faces.

- [donjon](https://donjon.bin.sh/) — The DM's utility belt: dungeon, world, inn, NPC, encounter and treasure generators. *Free.*
- [Azgaar's Fantasy Map Generator](https://azgaar.github.io/Fantasy-Map-Generator/) — Interactive, deeply customizable world maps, exportable as SVG/PNG. *Free, open source.*
- [Watabou — Procgen Arcana](https://watabou.github.io/) — Oleg Dolya's generator hub: cities, villages, dungeons, caves, regions. *Free.*
- [Medieval Fantasy City Generator](https://watabou.itch.io/medieval-fantasy-city-generator) — Watabou's city generator, with PNG/SVG/JSON export. *Free.*
- [One Page Dungeon](https://watabou.itch.io/one-page-dungeon) — Watabou's procedural one-page dungeon generator. *Free.*
- [This Person Does Not Exist](https://thispersondoesnotexist.com/) — A photoreal face per refresh: instant NPC portraits (modern-looking — best for urban/modern games or as a base to restyle). *Free.*
- Inkarnate and Dungeon Scrawl — for maps you draw rather than generate; listed under [Maps](#maps).
